🎮 Controls that matter

  • CTRL on ground: Monster Whisperer.
  • CTRL airborne: Aetherborne. Space flies up; Shift flies down; CTRL exits.
  • Double / Triple Jump: Jump → release Jump → press Jump again in midair. Level III allows one more release + Jump for the third jump.
  • Crouch + Jump: Voidstep.
  • Noclip: normal movement; hold Jump to rise and Shift/Crouch to descend through floors.
  • Pocket Dimension: press K while wearing the enchanted chestplate. The key can be rebound in Minecraft Controls.
  • Recall: while crouching, press F / Swap Hands to call your remembered Recall mount.
  • Homeward: sneak + right-click a Bed or Respawn Anchor with the enchanted compass to bind home.
  • Wayfinder: sneak + right-click a block with the enchanted compass to bind a waypoint.

🧩 Conflict & priority rules

  • Aetherborne temporarily owns airborne movement over Double Jump, Wallrunner, and Featherburst.
  • Lifebond will not activate while using an item/shield, holding Excavator II+, using Gravitation, or during Aetherborne flight/descent.
  • Deadeye ↔ Homing: incompatible.
  • Retrieval ↔ Phase Shot: incompatible.
  • Jackpot ↔ Spoilsurge: incompatible.
  • Guardian Bond ↔ Sacrificial Pact: mutually exclusive. You protect your pets, or your pets protect you — never both.
  • Loot priority: Essence Conversion → Jackpot → Spoilsurge.

📚 Book & anvil compatibility

  • Custom books only apply to their assigned item type. A helmet-only enchantment cannot be forced onto boots, a Saddle enchantment cannot go on armor, etc.
  • Combined enchanted books are allowed, but they do not bypass compatibility. If a stacked custom book contains even one enchantment that is invalid for the target item, the entire custom-book application is rejected.
  • When the same enchantment could be detected at multiple strengths, the highest effective level wins; weaker copies do not stack a second copy of the effect unless that mechanic explicitly says otherwise.

🐾 Pets, mounts & storage

  • Twin Blessing: the enchanted hoe only needs to be somewhere in hotbar slots 1–9 when the breeding baby is created.
  • Stablemaster / Recall: enchant a Saddle; Horse Armor enchantments such as Warhorse/Surefooted remain separate.
  • Pocket Dimension: storage belongs to the specific chestplate. Upgrading levels expands capacity without intentionally clearing the stored items.
  • Pacifist: protects tamed pets only; wild/passive food animals remain attackable.

Applies to: ChestplateLevels: I–III
TableTradeLoot
How to use it

Wear the enchanted chestplate while you own tamed pets.

What it does

The evil opposite of Guardian Bond: damage aimed at you is redirected into your tamed animals. I redirects a smaller share; III can redirect nearly all survivable damage when you have enough pets. Multiple pets split the burden, dramatically increasing your possible survival.

Important: Pets can die protecting you—including pets left at home. Known unloaded pets can receive deferred damage when they load again. Mutually incompatible with Guardian Bond.
