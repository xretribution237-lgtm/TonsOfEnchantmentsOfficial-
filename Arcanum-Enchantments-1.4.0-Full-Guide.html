✨ Arcanum Enchantments

A complete player-facing guide to all 130 custom enchantments: what they go on, exactly what you need to do to activate them, what happens when they trigger, how the controls interact, and where the books can be obtained.

Important: Remove older Arcanum JARs before installing 1.4.0. This guide describes Arcanum 1.4.0. Modded-content support is best-effort when third-party mods use standard Minecraft/Forge systems.

🎮 Controls that matter

  • CTRL on ground: Monster Whisperer.
  • CTRL airborne: Aetherborne.
  • Crouch + Jump: Voidstep.
  • Hold crouch grounded: Lifebond charge/field; Ghoststep and Ore Seeker may also function.
  • Crouch while falling: Featherburst, unless Aetherborne owns descent.
  • Right-click mature crops: Harvest Moon.

🧩 Conflict rules

  • Aetherborne temporarily owns airborne movement over Double Jump, Wallrunner, and Featherburst.
  • Lifebond will not activate while using an item/shield, holding Excavator II+, using Gravitation, or during Aetherborne flight/descent.
  • Deadeye ↔ Homing: incompatible.
  • Retrieval ↔ Phase Shot: incompatible.
  • Jackpot ↔ Spoilsurge: incompatible.
  • Loot priority: Essence Conversion → Jackpot → Spoilsurge.

📦 What is inside

Arcanum adds combat chains, lifesteal, executions, armor shredding, summons, survival flight, underwater sanctuaries, Bedrock mining, area mining, tree felling, auto-smelting, projectile steering, shield parries/reflection, fishing upgrades, loot multipliers, death protection, XP preservation, storage radar, healing auras, and ultra-rare Mythic books. Enchanted books use normal Minecraft enchanted-book visuals. Cataclysm is intentionally not included.

Install / update

  1. Use Minecraft 26.2 with Forge 65.1.2+ and Java 25.
  2. Remove older Arcanum JARs from the mods folder.
  3. Place the Arcanum 1.4.0 JAR in mods.
  4. Fully restart Minecraft. Back up important worlds before major mod updates.

👁️ Mythic & Summons

Aetherborne I

Applies to: BootsLevels: I
Boss Drop
How to use it

Wear the boots, jump/fall so you are airborne, then press CTRL. Use Space to fly up and Shift to fly down. Press CTRL again to exit flight.

What it does

Temporarily grants real creative-style flight in Survival. Exiting enters a protected slow descent; fall distance is repeatedly cleared, and landing after the descent grants Regeneration II for exactly 5 seconds.

Important: Creative/Spectator flight remains vanilla-owned and is never revoked. While Aetherborne owns flight/descent, Double Jump, Wallrunner, and Featherburst are suspended.
How to obtain

Ender Dragon: I 0.1%

Call of the Dead I

Applies to: ArmorLevels: I
Boss Drop
How to use it

Wear any armor piece carrying Call of the Dead. Remove it to return to normal hostility.

What it does

Hostile/angry mobs stop considering you a valid target. Creepers are defused, persistent anger is cleared, and mob-originated damage has a defensive failsafe. PvP is not disabled.

Important: Ultra-Mythic Warden drop. “Must’ve been the wind.”

💀 Utility & Durability

Lasting Will I

Applies to: Durability itemsLevels: I
TableTradeLoot
How to use it

Use the enchanted durability item normally until it would break.

What it does

Instead of breaking, the item survives at exactly 1 durability. At 1 durability it is locked from normal attacking, mining, and right-click use until repaired.

Important: Lifebond repair can restore durability and unlock it again.
How to obtain

Enchanting Table; Villager Trade; Random Enchanted Loot

Soulbound I

Applies to: Items that can take vanishing-type enchantsLevels: I
Loot
How to use it

Have Soulbound on the item when you die.

What it does

The item is removed from normal death drops, saved by the mod, and returned to your inventory after respawn.

How to obtain

Random Enchanted Loot
